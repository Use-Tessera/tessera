Signer Coordinator Security model

Would your signers sign this?

Every Tessera signer decodes what it is asked to sign and refuses anything its policy doesn't allow. Paste a policy and a transaction, and this page runs the signer's own policy code on them, here in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere.

Policy

The format of a signer's policy.toml. Anything not allowed is refused.

Expiry is checked against the current time. The ledger is only used for authorization entries.
